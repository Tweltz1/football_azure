Store layout

Which aisle each kind of product is in. Shop Stride uses this instead of shopper guesses and routes shoppers through your store. Paste it below or upload a spreadsheet saved as CSV. Any of these work:

Aisle 7: cereal, oatmeal, pop-tarts
Aisle 12: frozen pizza, ice cream
Produce: apples, bananas, salad

or two columns, Category,Aisle (for example Cereal,7). Saving replaces the whole layout, so include every aisle each time.

This week's deals

Items on your weekly ad. Shoppers who have them on their list see an On sale badge. One item per line: item, price, end date. With no end date a deal runs 7 days. Saving replaces last week's list. Save an empty box to clear it.
