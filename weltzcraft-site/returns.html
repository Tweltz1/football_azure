Returns and Shipping

Weltzcraft merch · Last updated November 21, 2026

Every item is printed or embroidered for you when you order, by our print partner Printful.

Shipping

  • We ship to US addresses only.
  • Shipping is $5.99 for the first item plus $1.50 for each extra item, up to $14.99 per order. You see the total at checkout.
  • Orders usually arrive in 5 to 10 business days after printing. You get an email with tracking when your order ships.

Damaged, misprinted or wrong items

If something arrives damaged, misprinted or isn't what you ordered, email sales@weltzcraft.com within 30 days of delivery. Include your order email and a photo of the problem. We'll send a replacement or refund the price of the item, your choice.

What we can't take back

Because each item is made to order, we don't accept returns or exchanges for the wrong size, a color you didn't like or a change of mind. Not sure about a size? Email us before you order and we'll help.

Shipping costs

Shipping costs aren't refunded. If we ask you to send an item back, you pay the return shipping.

Canceling an order

Email us right away. Orders go to print quickly, but if yours hasn't started printing yet we'll cancel it and refund you in full.
