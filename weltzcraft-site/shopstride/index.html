Shop Stride

Your grocery list, in the order you walk the store.

Get it on the App Store

Type your list, pick your store, and Shop Stride gives you the shortest walk through it, with aisles filled in and frozen food saved for last. Save recipes, share your list with the family, and borrow recipes from the community.

What it does

  • The shortest route through your store, aisle by aisle
  • Frozen and cold food saved for the end of the trip
  • Recipes that add their ingredients to your list
  • A family list everyone can add to
  • Community recipes by category
