ToteTrack Privacy Policy

ToteTrack by Weltzcraft Studios · Last updated November 11, 2026

ToteTrack does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • Everything you enter (totes, items, locations, notes and photos) is stored only on your device.
  • There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.
  • The camera is used only to scan QR labels and, if you choose, to take photos of your totes. Photos stay on your device.
  • Printing and PDF sharing happen through iOS. ToteTrack does not send your labels anywhere.
  • ToteTrack Pro subscriptions are bought and managed through Apple. ToteTrack never sees your payment details; the app only asks Apple, on your phone, whether your subscription is active.

Deleting the app deletes all of your ToteTrack data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
