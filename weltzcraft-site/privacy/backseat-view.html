Backseat View Privacy Policy

Backseat View by Weltzcraft Studios · Last updated November 11, 2026

Backseat View does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.
  • If you enter a camera's snapshot address, your phone loads images directly from that camera. Images are shown on your phone and are not sent to us or saved.
  • The CarPlay widget shows a picture built into the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
