Hood Protocol Privacy Policy

Hood Protocol by Weltzcraft Studios · Last updated November 11, 2026

Hood Protocol is a party game you can play alone with bots, with friends nearby, or online. You can play without an account.

Without an account

  • Your games, wins and look are saved only on your phone.
  • Nearby games connect phones directly over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, with no internet.
  • Online games pass game messages through our server using a room code. Messages are encrypted for each player and aren't stored.

With an account

  • If you sign in with email or Google, we store your email address, the display name you choose, your look, and your games played and wins. Your name and wins can appear on the leaderboard.
  • Your sign-in is shared with Hood League, another Weltzcraft Studios app.
  • You can delete your Hood Protocol profile and stats any time from the app.

There is no advertising, and we don't sell your information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
