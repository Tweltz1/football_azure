Your camera is ready

The Backseat View camera is set up and will join your iPhone's hotspot whenever it's switched on.

  1. Unplug it from the computer and plug in the power bank.
  2. Make sure Personal Hotspot is on (with Maximize Compatibility).
  3. Open Backseat View on your iPhone and tap Watch Back Seat.
