Set up the Backseat View camera

A small rechargeable camera for the back seat. It sends the picture and the baby's sound to the Backseat View app on your iPhone, which puts a baby card on your CarPlay screen.

What to buy (about $50)

Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32S3 Sense
The camera. Get the Sense version (it comes with the camera and microphone board). Search Amazon or seeedstudio.com.		$15–25
Small power bank with a built-in USB-C plug
For example the Anker Nano Power Bank (5,000 mAh). It plugs straight into the camera, so there's no cable and no soldering. That gives about 12–15 hours per charge.		$20–25
Headrest mount or a strap
Anything that holds the camera and power bank on the back of the front seat, facing the baby. Velcro works too.		$5–10
USB-C cable
Only for the one-time setup on a computer. It must be a data cable (most phone cables are).		—

When it arrives, press the little camera board onto the back of the main board until it clicks. The gold connector goes on the B2B socket.

One-time setup (5 minutes)

  1. On your iPhone, open Settings > Personal Hotspot. Turn on Allow Others to Join and Maximize Compatibility (the camera needs that one). Note the Wi-Fi name and password shown there.
  2. On a Windows or Mac computer, open this page in Chrome or Edge.
  3. Plug the camera into the computer with the USB-C cable, then click Install. Pick the port that appears (often "USB JTAG/serial debug unit") and click Install again.
  4. When it asks for Wi-Fi, type your iPhone's hotspot name and password from step 1.
This browser can't install it. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Open this page from https://weltzcraft.com to install.

If the camera won't show up: hold the tiny B (boot) button, plug it in, let go, and try Install again.

Every drive

  1. Plug the power bank into the camera. The orange light blinks while it looks for your hotspot and goes dark when it's ready.
  2. Open Backseat View on your iPhone and tap Watch Back Seat. Then lock your phone.
  3. The baby card shows on your CarPlay Dashboard. You'll hear the baby through the car speakers, and it chimes if the baby starts crying.

Changing the Wi-Fi later

For 3 minutes after switching the camera on, it also makes a Wi-Fi network called Backseat Camera Setup. Join it from your phone, open http://192.168.4.1 and type the new network name and password. You can also run Install on this page again.

Glance only, like a mirror. Not a medical or safety device. Never leave a child alone in a car. Mount the camera securely so it can't come loose.
