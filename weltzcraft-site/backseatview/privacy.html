Backseat View Privacy Policy

Backseat View by Weltzcraft Studios · Last updated November 22, 2026

Backseat View does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.
  • The camera (the Backseat View mini camera, or an old iPhone or iPad) sends its picture and sound straight to your own iPhone over your hotspot or a direct phone-to-phone link. Nothing goes through the internet or to us, and nothing is recorded or saved.
  • Crying and movement are worked out on your iPhone with Apple's built-in sound recognition.
  • The baby card on your Lock Screen and CarPlay screen shows a small picture from the camera. It stays on your phone.
  • Camera and microphone access are only used when a device is set as the camera.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
