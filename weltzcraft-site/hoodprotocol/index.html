Hood Protocol

A hidden-role party game for grown-up friend groups.

Hood Protocol is a hidden-role party game. Somebody at the table is a Narc. Play with bots, with phones in the same room (no internet needed), or online with a room code.

What it does

  • Secret roles dealt to every phone
  • Play with bots when the group is small
  • Same-room play over local wireless, no internet needed
  • Online rooms with a code
  • Dress up your character
