Weltzcraft Studios Privacy Policy

weltzcraft.com and the Weltzcraft merch store · Last updated November 22, 2026

This page covers the weltzcraft.com website, the project form and the merch store. Each Weltzcraft app has its own privacy policy, linked at the bottom of this page.

Browsing the site

  • The site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • When you click certain buttons (like Contact us, Merch or an App Store link) the page sends an anonymous click count with no ID, cookie or personal details. Cloudflare, which hosts the site, keeps standard request logs.
  • The free sample site builder sends what you type (your business details, and a Facebook page or business name if you give one) to our server to build your preview. We use Google's Gemini service to write the words and choose photos, and may look up public details about the business online. We keep the preview so your link works; the link stops working after 30 days.
  • The builder asks for your email. We use it to send you the link to your sample site, and we may send one follow-up asking how it went. Every follow-up has a link to stop further emails. We don't add you to a mailing list or share your email.

The project form

If you send a project brief, we get your name, email address, company (if you add one) and what you wrote. It is emailed to sales@weltzcraft.com through Resend so we can reply. We use it only to talk with you about your project and don't add you to a mailing list.

Buying a website

  • Stripe takes your payment on its own secure checkout page and collects your name, email, phone number, billing address and card. We never see your full card number.
  • Your name, email, phone and address are used to register your web address in your name through Cloudflare Registrar, with private WHOIS, and we keep them so we can run your site and contact you about it.
  • The customer portal signs you in with a link sent to your email. Photos you upload and changes you make are stored so your site can show them.
  • When a visitor books a time, places an order or sends a message on a website we built, those details are emailed to that business and kept so the business can see them.

Buying merch

  • Stripe takes your payment on its own secure checkout page and collects your name, email, phone number, shipping address and card. We never see your full card number. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Printful prints and ships your order, so it gets your name, shipping address, phone, email and what you ordered. See Printful's privacy policy.
  • We get the order details from Stripe and Printful and email you order and shipping updates from sales@weltzcraft.com through Resend.
  • We keep order records as long as tax and accounting rules require.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information, share it for advertising, or send marketing email unless you ask for it.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we have about you, email sales@weltzcraft.com. We'll answer within 30 days. We may need to keep order records the law requires.

Children

The site and store aren't directed at children under 13.
