Hood League

A live auction draft room for your fantasy football league.

Open Hood League

Hood League runs your fantasy football auction draft live: every manager bids from their own phone, the TV shows the board, and the room keeps the budget math for you.

What it does

  • Live auction draft room with a nomination clock and bid buttons
  • Free mock draft against bots, no sign-in needed
  • TV view for draft night, so the whole room can follow along
  • Player rankings and auction values that update through the season
  • Draft history for every league, year after year
  • Start/Sit chat for lineup calls
