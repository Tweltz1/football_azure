Snap the couch on your curb. Neighbors come get it.

Get it on the App Store

Curbsy is for free stuff near you. Snap a photo of what you are giving away and the phone names it for you. Neighbors see what is free nearby and tap "I’m on my way."

What it does

  • Post in seconds: the camera names the item and picks a category
  • See free stuff nearby, closest or newest first
  • "I’m on my way" so the giver knows someone is coming, with a rough arrival time and never your location
  • Stars after each pickup, pickup counts and a Verified badge, so you know who you’re meeting
  • Share any listing to a Facebook group or a text
  • "Looking for" posts: ask your neighbors for something you need
