Why do I need to sign in?

Your totes, items, packing lists and photos are saved to your account, so they are there on any iPhone you sign in on. You can sign in with Apple (and hide your email) or Google.

What does ToteTrack Pro add?

The free version holds 10 totes. ToteTrack Pro adds unlimited totes and Snap to add (take a photo of an open tote and ToteTrack lists what's inside). Pro is $1.99 a month or $14.99 a year.

Managing or cancelling Pro

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. To cancel or change your plan, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.

Got a new phone? Open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases. Have a promo code? Tap Have a promo code? on the Pro screen.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Account & backup, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything saved to it, and removes it from your phone.
