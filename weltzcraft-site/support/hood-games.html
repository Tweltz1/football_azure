Hood Games Support

Hood Games by Weltzcraft Studios

Hood Games is a hub of party games, Hood Quest tabletop adventures and draft mini-games, with your characters and scores saved to one sign-in.

Contact us

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email us and a real person replies, usually within one business day.

hoodgames@weltzcraft.com

Do I need an account?

Sign in with Apple, Google or email to join leagues and save your profile, characters and scores. Many games can also be played as a guest.

Playing with friends

Start a game and share its code; friends join from their own phones. Some games also work passed around one phone.

Deleting your account

On the Hood Games home screen, tap Delete account under your name. Your sign-in and saved profile are deleted for good. Hood League, Hood Games and Curbsy share one sign-in, so it is removed from all of them.
