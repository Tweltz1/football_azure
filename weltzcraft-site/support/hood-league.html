Hood League by Weltzcraft Studios

Hood League runs fantasy football auction drafts, with offline drafts between nearby phones, Start/Sit and trade help, and rankings.

Contact us

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email us and a real person replies, usually within one business day.

hoodgames@weltzcraft.com

Do I need an account?

Sign in with Apple, Google or email to join leagues and save your profile, characters and scores. Many games can also be played as a guest.

Drafting without internet

Hood League phones in the same room can run a draft together over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth with no internet. Allow Local Network access when asked.

Deleting your account

Open Profile, then tap Delete account. Your sign-in and saved profile are deleted for good. Hood League, Hood Games and Curbsy share one sign-in, so it is removed from all of them.
