# ToteTrack Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 8, 2026_

ToteTrack does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- Everything you enter (totes, items, locations, notes and photos) is stored only on your device.
- There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.
- The camera is used only to scan QR labels and, if you choose, to take photos of your totes.
  Photos stay on your device.
- Printing and PDF sharing happen through iOS. ToteTrack does not send your labels anywhere.

- ToteTrack Pro subscriptions are bought and managed through Apple. ToteTrack never sees your payment
  details; the app only asks Apple's StoreKit, on your phone, whether your subscription is active.

Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Tweltz1/football_azure/issues
