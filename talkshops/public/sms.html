Text message opt-in

Talk Shops (talkshops.app) · Toll-free number +1 833-897-8998

Auto repair shops use Talk Shops to text their own customers about a vehicle the customer has dropped off or booked for service. Texts are transactional only: check-in confirmation, repair progress, estimates to approve, pickup and appointment reminders, and replies to the customer's questions. No marketing.

How customers opt in

1. At the shop counter (check-in). When the customer drops off their vehicle, staff enter the customer's mobile number in the Talk Shops check-in form and read the disclosure below. Staff check the consent box only if the customer agrees. Without the box checked, Talk Shops sends no texts for that vehicle.

Read to the customer before checking the box: “Ridgeline Auto will text you updates about your vehicle: check-in, progress, estimates to approve and pickup. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Privacy: talkshops.app/privacy. Terms: talkshops.app/terms.”

2. Online booking. On a shop's Talk Shops booking page, the customer enters their mobile number and checks this box themselves. It is not pre-checked.

Agreeing to texts is never a condition of any purchase. Phone numbers are never bought, rented or shared for marketing.

What customers receive

The first text names the shop and links to the customer's private vehicle page:

Ridgeline Auto has your 2012 Chevrolet Malibu. Track it here: https://talkshops.app/s/hkeyfxeqbk7z Reply STOP to opt out
Ridgeline Auto: Your 2012 Chevrolet Malibu estimate ($813.00) is ready to approve: https://talkshops.app/s/hkeyfxeqbk7z Reply STOP to opt out.
  • Message frequency: varies with the repair, usually a few texts per visit.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt out: reply STOP to any text. You'll get one confirmation and no more texts. Reply START to resume.
  • Help: reply HELP, or email support@talkshops.app.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties. See the Privacy Policy and Terms (text message terms are in the Text message terms section).
