Curbsy Help

Curbsy is an iPhone app for giving away and finding free stuff near you.

Contact us

Open the app and go to Me > Contact Curbsy. A person reads every message within 24 hours. To report a listing, tap the flag on its page. To report a person, tap the flag in the chat.

Posting

Tap +, take a photo, and check the name Curbsy suggests. You see a preview before anything is shared. Your address is never shown: neighbors see only how far away the item is, and the exact spot only once they are on their way or you write back to them.

Picking something up

Tap I'm on my way to hold an item while you drive over. Keep location on for Curbsy during the trip. If you stop heading there, the item goes back up for someone else. Tap Got it once it's loaded.

Curbsy Plus and tips

Curbsy Plus is an optional monthly subscription billed by Apple. To cancel, open iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Curbsy. To get it back on a new phone, go to Me > Restore purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Warnings, hidden listings and bans

If a listing of yours was hidden or your account was paused and you think it was a mistake, tap Appeal in the Me tab or Ask for a review on the listing.

Deleting your account

Go to Me > Delete my account. Your listings, photos and messages are deleted right away. Cancel Curbsy Plus first, since Apple bills it separately.
