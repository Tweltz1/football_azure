Store manager sign-in

Sign in with your email. We'll send you a 6-digit code. Use your work email if you have one; it helps us confirm you work at the store.

What you can do

Once we confirm you work at the store, you can keep it current in Shop Stride: which aisle items are in, this week's sales, items that are out of stock, and a short note for shoppers like holiday hours. Shoppers see your changes right away.

Items & aisles

Where things are in your store. Shop Stride uses this instead of shopper guesses. A line can be a category ("cereal") or one product ("Pop-Tarts"); the most specific match wins.

Replace everything from a spreadsheet

Paste it below or upload a spreadsheet saved as CSV. This replaces the whole list. Any of these work:

Aisle 7: cereal, oatmeal, pop-tarts
Aisle 12: frozen pizza, ice cream
Produce: apples, bananas, salad

or two columns, Category,Aisle (for example Cereal,7).

Sales this week

Shoppers with the item on their list see an On sale tag, and everyone who picks your store can browse all your sales in the app and add them to their list. With no end date a sale runs 7 days.

Replace all sales from a spreadsheet

One item per line: item, price, end date. This replaces the current sales. Save an empty box to clear them.

Out of stock

Shoppers with the item on their list see "Out of stock here", so they can plan ahead. Add the date it's back if you know it; with no date it shows for 7 days.

Store news

A short note shoppers see when they pick your store, like holiday hours or "Fresh sweet corn is in!". Up to 3 lines, 280 characters.

For developers: send it from your own system

The same data can be sent by API, for example from your weekly ad system. Keep your key private; anyone with it can change your store's data.

          "rows": [{"item": "Milk", "price": "$2.99", "ends": "2026-11-15"}] instead of csv,
          add "check": true to test without saving, or send {"action": "store"} to read back what's saved.
