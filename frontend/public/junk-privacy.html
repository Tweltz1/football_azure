Curbsy Privacy Policy

Curbsy lets neighbors give away things they don't need, for free. This page explains what the app collects and why. Last updated November 10, 2026.

What we collect

  • Account. When you sign in with Apple or Google we receive your name, email address and an account ID. Other people only see your first name or display name.
  • Location. Your phone's location is used to show items near you. When you post an item, its location is saved rounded to about 100 meters. Other people only see how far away an item is, never its address or exact spot. Browsing doesn't save your location on our servers.
  • "I'm on my way" trips. When you tap I'm on my way, the app sends your location about once a minute until you pick the item up, cancel, or the claim ends, including while the app is in the background (iOS shows the blue location indicator). We use it only to check you're heading to the item, and save only your distance to it. The person giving the item away sees that distance and a rough arrival time, never where you are. Tracking stops as soon as the trip ends.
  • Photos and listings. The photos, titles, descriptions and pickup notes you post are public to anyone using the app.
  • Messages. Messages you send about an item are stored so you and the other person can read them.

Automatic item detection

When you add the first photo to a new listing, the app looks at it on your phone with Apple's built-in image recognition to suggest a name, category and search words. This happens on the device and the photo isn't sent anywhere for it. You can change anything it suggests.

Helping item recognition get better

When you change a name or category the app suggested, the app sends us what it guessed and what you changed it to (for example, "suitcase" changed to "tape measure"), linked to your account. We use these fixes only to train Curbsy's own item recognition, which ships in future versions of the app.

If you turn on Help improve item recognition in the Me tab (it's off unless you turn it on), the app also sends a small picture of just that one item, cut from your photo. Your full photo and your location are never sent for this. Turn the switch off any time to stop. Deleting your account deletes your fixes and these pictures too.

How we use it

Only to run the app: show listings near you, let people message each other, and keep the app safe (for example, hiding listings that get reported). We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, show ads, or track you across other apps.

Where it's stored

In the app's database and photo storage hosted by Supabase.

Deleting your data

Delete a listing any time from the listing itself. To delete your whole account, open the app, tap Me, then Delete my account. That removes your listings, photos, messages, item recognition fixes and sign-in right away.
