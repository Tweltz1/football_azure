Curbsy Privacy Policy

Curbsy lets neighbors give away things they don't need, for free. This page explains what the app collects and why. Last updated November 8, 2026.

What we collect

  • Account. When you sign in with Apple, Google or GitHub we receive your name, email address and an account ID. Other people only see your first name or display name.
  • Location. Your phone's location is used to show items near you. When you post an item, its location is saved rounded to about 100 meters. Other people only see how far away an item is, never its address or exact spot. Browsing doesn't save your location on our servers.
  • Photos and listings. The photos, titles, descriptions and pickup notes you post are public to anyone using the app.
  • Messages. Messages you send about an item are stored so you and the other person can read them.

Automatic item detection

When you add the first photo to a new listing, the photo is sent to Google's Gemini service to suggest a name, category, description and search words, and to check that the photo is allowed. You can change anything it suggests. Google processes the photo under its API terms.

How we use it

Only to run the app: show listings near you, let people message each other, and keep the app safe (for example, hiding listings that get reported). We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, show ads, or track you across other apps.

Where it's stored

In the app's database and photo storage hosted by Supabase.

Deleting your data

Delete a listing any time from the listing itself. To delete your whole account, open the app, tap Me, then Delete my account. That removes your listings, photos, messages and sign-in right away.
