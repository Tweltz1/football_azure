Hood League Privacy Policy

Hood League is a private fantasy football auction draft site for a group of friends.

What we collect. When you sign in with GitHub or Google we receive your name, email address and an account ID. We also store the leagues, teams, bids and chat messages you create on the site.

How we use it. Only to sign you in, show your name to the other people in your league and run the draft. We don't sell or share your information, and we don't show ads.

Where it's stored. In the site's database hosted by Supabase.

Deleting your data. Ask the league commissioner to remove you, or contact the site owner and we'll delete your account and data.
