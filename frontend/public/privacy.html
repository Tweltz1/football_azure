Hood League and Hood Games Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Hood League is a fantasy football auction draft site and app. Hood Games is its games hub: card and party games, Hood Quest adventures and draft mini-games. Both are made by Weltzcraft Studios and share one sign-in. This policy covers both, on the web and in the iPhone apps.

What we collect. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address, profile picture and an account ID. When you sign up with email we keep your email address, the name you give, and a scrambled (hashed) copy of your password. We store what you create: leagues, teams, bids and chat messages, plus game rooms, characters, campaigns and scores. You can also play as a guest or offline; then your data stays on your device.

Start/Sit chat and Hood Quest. Questions you type in Start/Sit chat, and what you say or type to the Hood Quest narrator, are sent to Google's Gemini AI service to write a reply. We save Start/Sit questions and answers to improve the feature. Don't put personal details in them.

Voice and video in games. If you turn on voice or camera in a game room, your audio and video go to the other players in that room over a direct connection, relayed through Cloudflare when needed. We don't record or store it. Speech-to-text for game transcripts runs on your device; only the text is sent to the room.

How we use it. Only to sign you in, show your name to the people you play with, and run drafts and games. We don't sell your information, and we don't show ads or use advertising trackers.

Where it's stored. In our database hosted by Supabase. The site is served by Cloudflare. Player and game data comes from public sports sources; we don't send them anything about you.

Google user data. From Google sign-in we only use your name, email address and profile picture, only for the purposes above. We don't use it for ads, don't sell it, and don't share it with anyone outside the site.

Children. Hood League and Hood Games are not meant for children under 13.

Deleting your data. Ask your league commissioner to remove you from a league, or email hoodgames@weltzcraft.com and we'll delete your account and data.
